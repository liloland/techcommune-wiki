Not sure what to add? Here are some prompts. Check the [wiki search](wiki:search) and the [site search](site:search.html) first, and add to an existing page if there is one.

## Fixes

- A problem that took you more than an hour to solve, and the one line that finally fixed it
- A fix for a confusing error message that other people are likely to search for
- An upgrade that went wrong, and how you recovered

## Tips

- A command or flag you wish you had known years earlier
- A shell alias or small script you use every day
- A better way to do something the main site shows the long way

## Experiences

- A mistake that taught you something, told without names
- What changed when you moved from one tool to another, and what you miss
- The checklist you now use because of an outage

## Ideas

- A tool or approach you have not tried yet but think is worth a look
- A question that has no single right answer, such as how people handle a particular problem

When you are ready, read [[how-to-contribute]] and send a pull request.
